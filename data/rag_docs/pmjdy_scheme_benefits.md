# Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) — Scheme Guidelines and Benefits

## Section 1: Core Objectives and Zero-Balance Accounts
**Source Citation:** [Source: PMJDY Guidelines, Sec 1.1]
Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is the National Mission for Financial Inclusion to ensure access to financial services, namely, basic savings & deposit accounts, remittance, credit, insurance, and pension in an affordable manner.
1. **Zero Minimum Balance:** Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts (BSBDA) opened under PMJDY do not require any minimum balance requirement. There are no penalty charges for maintaining a zero balance.
2. **Interest on Deposits:** Normal savings bank interest rates apply on deposits maintained in PMJDY accounts.

## Section 2: Overdraft (OD) Facility
**Source Citation:** [Source: PMJDY Scheme Rules, Sec 2.4]
1. **Overdraft Limit:** An overdraft (OD) facility up to **₹10,000** is available to eligible PMJDY account holders.
2. **Eligibility Criteria:**
   - Satisfactory operation of the account for at least 6 months.
   - One account per household (preferably the woman of the house).
   - Age criteria: 18 years to 65 years.
   - For OD amounts up to ₹2,000, no conditions/guarantees are attached.
3. **Interest Rates on Overdraft:** Overdraft interest is charged at reasonable base rates (MCLR + nominal margin) strictly on the utilized amount for the utilized days.

## Section 3: RuPay Debit Card and Built-in Insurance Cover
**Source Citation:** [Source: PMJDY Insurance Terms, Sec 3.2]
1. **Accident Insurance Cover:** RuPay Debit Card issued to PMJDY account holders comes with built-in accidental insurance cover:
   - For accounts opened after 28.08.2018, accidental death and total disability cover is **₹2,00,000** (₹2 Lakh).
2. **Card Usage Condition:** To keep the accidental insurance cover active, the RuPay card must have been used for at least one successful financial or non-financial transaction at an ATM, PoS terminal, micro-ATM, or e-commerce merchant within 90 days prior to the date of accident.
3. **No Premium Charged to Customer:** The insurance premium is fully sponsored under the national scheme and not deducted from the account balance.

## Section 4: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
**Source Citation:** [Source: PMJDY Implementation Manual, Sec 4.1]
PMJDY accounts serve as the primary vehicle for government Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT) including PM-KISAN, LPG subsidy (PAHAL), MNREGA wages, and scholarship schemes. Accounts linked with Aadhaar facilitate seamless, leak-proof digital transfers directly into beneficiaries' accounts.
