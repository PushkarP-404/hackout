# Reserve Bank of India (RBI) — Regulatory Framework for Digital Lending

## Section 1: Scope and Applicability
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines 2022, Sec 1.1]
These guidelines apply to all commercial banks, primary (urban) co-operative banks, state co-operative banks, district central co-operative banks, and non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) engaged in digital lending. Digital lending refers to a remote and automated lending process largely by use of seamless digital technologies for customer acquisition, credit assessment, loan approval, disbursement, recovery, and associated customer service.

## Section 2: Key Fact Statement (KFS) and APR Disclosure
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines 2022, Sec 2.3]
1. **Mandatory KFS:** Regulated Entities (REs) must provide a Key Fact Statement (KFS) to the borrower before the execution of the loan contract in a standardized format.
2. **Annual Percentage Rate (APR):** APR must represent the all-inclusive cost of digital loans to the borrower, incorporating the cost of funds, credit assessment charges, processing fees, and all other recurring or non-recurring charges.
3. **No Hidden Costs:** Any fee or charge not explicitly disclosed in the KFS cannot be levied on the borrower at any stage of the loan tenure.
4. **Vernacular KFS:** The KFS must be delivered to the borrower in a language understood by them (English, Hindi, or primary regional language).

## Section 3: Cooling-Off / Look-Up Period
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines 2022, Sec 3.1]
1. **Definition of Cooling-Off Period:** A cooling-off / look-up period is the time window given to borrowers to exit a digital loan without paying any penal interest or foreclosure penalty.
2. **Duration:** 
   - For loans having tenure of 7 days or more: Minimum cooling-off period is **3 calendar days**.
   - For loans having tenure of less than 7 days: Minimum cooling-off period is **1 calendar day**.
3. **Exit Terms:** If the borrower decides to exit within the cooling-off period, they are only required to repay the principal amount disbursed along with proportionate APR accrued during that period. No prepayment, foreclosure, or cancellation penalty can be charged.

## Section 4: Direct Loan Disbursal and Repayments
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines 2022, Sec 4.2]
1. **Direct Bank-to-Bank Transfer:** All loan disbursements must be executed directly into the verified bank account of the borrower. Under no circumstances may disbursals pass through pass-through or pool accounts of third-party Lending Service Providers (LSPs).
2. **Repayments:** All repayments must be directly executed from the borrower's bank account to the regulated entity's balance sheet account without intermediate pooling by app aggregators.

## Section 5: Recovery Agents and Ethical Conduct
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines 2022, Sec 5.4]
1. **Authorized Channels Only:** REs and their LSPs shall strictly communicate with borrowers through officially designated telephone numbers and customer service portals.
2. **Timing of Contact:** Borrowers shall not be contacted before 08:00 AM or after 07:00 PM for loan recovery.
3. **Prohibition of Harassment:** Regulated entities are strictly prohibited from using intimidation, public shaming, accessing borrower phone contact lists, accessing media/photos, or unauthorized physical visits.

## Section 6: Data Privacy and Storage Restrictions
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines 2022, Sec 6.1]
1. **Data Minimization:** Lending apps shall collect only such data as is strictly necessary for credit assessment and KYC (e.g., PAN, last 4 digits of Aadhaar with consent).
2. **No Access to Mobile Hardware:** Apps are strictly forbidden from accessing mobile device storage, contact lists, call logs, or camera/microphone without explicit one-time session consent for video KYC.
3. **Data Localization:** All digital lending data must reside on servers physically located within India.
