# BharatBanker AI — Responsible Lending & Affordability Guidelines

## Section 1: Debt-to-Income (DTI) Safety Thresholds
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Credit Assessment & Affordability Framework, Sec 1.3]
1. **The 50% DTI Safety Cap:** In accordance with responsible lending standards, total monthly debt obligations (existing EMIs plus proposed new EMI) shall not exceed **50% of the applicant's verified net monthly income**.
2. **Hard Veto on Over-Indebtedness:** When an applicant's projected DTI exceeds 50%, further credit expansion is blocked to safeguard the borrower from falling into a debt trap.
3. **Affordable Alternative Guidance:** Rather than a punitive decline, the system provides transparent feedback advising the borrower on the maximum affordable loan amount within safe DTI limits.

## Section 2: Empathetic Restructuring and Moratorium Support
**Source Citation:** [Source: BharatBanker Fair Recovery & Financial Health Code, Sec 2.1]
1. **Zero-Penalty Rescheduling:** Borrowers encountering sudden financial stress (such as medical emergencies or seasonal income dips) can apply for a 30-day EMI pause or tenure restructuring without negative credit score reporting or penal charges.
2. **Financial Counselling:** Bank financial health counsellors provide free assistance to restructure high-interest obligations into sustainable repayment schedules.
