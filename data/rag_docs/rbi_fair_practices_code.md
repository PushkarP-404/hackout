# Reserve Bank of India (RBI) — Fair Practices Code for Lenders

## Section 1: Loan Application and Sanction Terms
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Fair Practices Code, Sec 1.2]
1. **Loan Application Acknowledgement:** Regulated lenders must issue an explicit acknowledgement for all loan applications along with a verifiable reference number and indicated timeline for decision.
2. **Clear Terms and Conditions:** The loan sanction letter must transparently state:
   - Sanctioned amount
   - Annualized interest rate (APR)
   - Method of application of interest
   - Repayment schedule, EMI amount, and tenure
   - All charges including penal charges and processing fees.

## Section 2: Foreclosure Charges and Prepayment Penalty Restrictions
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Master Direction on Fair Practices, Sec 2.5]
1. **Floating Rate Personal / Home Loans:** Banks and NBFCs shall **NOT charge foreclosure charges or pre-payment penalties** on any floating-rate term loans sanctioned to individual borrowers for purposes other than business.
2. **Right to Prepay:** Borrowers have the unconditional right to prepay part or all of their loan balance at any time without penalty for individual floating-rate loans.

## Section 3: Penal Charges vs. Penal Interest (Fair Lending Conduct)
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Guidelines on Fair Lending Conduct — Penal Charges, Sec 3.1]
1. **No Capitalization of Penal Charges:** Penal charges for delayed payments or non-compliance must be levied purely as 'penal charges' and shall not be compounded or added to the principal balance for charging further interest.
2. **Reasonable Quantum:** Penal charges must be reasonable, proportionate to the default, and clearly disclosed upfront in the Key Fact Statement without punitive escalation.

## Section 4: What Happens When a Borrower Cannot Repay (Financial Distress Protocol)
**Source Citation:** [Source: RBI Framework for Stressed Assets and Grievance Redressal, Sec 4.4]
1. **Early Stress Identification:** Lenders are instructed to identify early indicators of financial distress and offer proactive restructuring or rescheduling options before categorizing the account as NPA.
2. **Empathetic Restructuring:** Borrowers facing genuine income disruption, health emergencies, or business distress can request EMI rescheduling, tenure extension, or a temporary moratorium without punitive harassment.
3. **Ombudsman Access:** In case of unfair recovery practices or arbitrary loan recall, borrowers have the statutory right to appeal directly to the RBI Banking Ombudsman under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.
