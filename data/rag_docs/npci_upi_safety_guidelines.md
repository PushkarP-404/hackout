# National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) — UPI Security and Dispute Guidelines

## Section 1: The UPI Golden Rule — PIN is for Sending, Never for Receiving
**Source Citation:** [Source: NPCI UPI Safety Advisory, Sec 1.1]
1. **UPI PIN Rule:** You need to enter your UPI PIN **ONLY when you are sending money / paying someone** or checking your account balance.
2. **Receiving Money Does Not Require a PIN:** To receive money or receive a refund, you **NEVER** need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code. Any message or person asking you to enter your PIN to receive funds is fraudulent.
3. **Collect Requests:** When receiving a collect request, check the sender's VPA, name, and the amount carefully. Approving a collect request and entering your PIN will instantly deduct money from your account.

## Section 2: Turnaround Time (TAT) and Auto-Reversal for Failed Transactions
**Source Citation:** [Source: NPCI Circular on Harmonisation of TAT, Sec 2.3]
1. **Auto-Reversal Timelines (T+1 Rule):** If an amount is debited from the customer's account during a UPI transaction but not credited to the beneficiary account due to technical failure or timeout:
   - The remitter bank must auto-reverse the funds within **T + 1 business day**.
2. **Compensation for Delay:** If the amount is not reversed within T + 1 day, the customer is entitled to compensation of **₹100 per day of delay** beyond T + 1 day, automatically credited to the customer's account without requiring a separate claim.

## Section 3: Dispute Resolution and Online Dispute Resolution (ODR)
**Source Citation:** [Source: NPCI UPI Procedural Guidelines, Sec 3.5]
1. **Filing Disputes in App:** Customers can raise a dispute directly within any UPI app (BHIM, PhonePe, Google Pay, Bank apps) under transaction history -> "Raise Dispute" or "Report Issue".
2. **Escalation Hierarchy:** If the app or PSP bank does not resolve the dispute within 30 days, the customer can escalate directly through NPCI's online dispute resolution portal or to the RBI Integrated Banking Ombudsman.

## Section 4: Safe Digital Payment Practices
**Source Citation:** [Source: NPCI Cyber Security Guidelines, Sec 4.2]
1. **Never Share OTP or MPIN:** Never share OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, or card CVV with anyone, including bank staff or customer care representatives. Banks never ask for your PIN.
2. **Beware of Screen Sharing Apps:** Never install remote access applications (such as AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport) at the instruction of unknown callers claiming to fix banking issues.
3. **Verify Payee Name:** UPI apps display the registered bank account name of the receiver before confirming payment. Always verify the recipient's verified name.
